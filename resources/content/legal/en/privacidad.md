## 1. What data we collect

For Cuotas to work, we store the information you enter and some basic technical data:

- **Account:** name, email, password (encrypted).
- **Cards and installments:** merchant name, amounts, dates, last 4 digits.
- **Payments:** subscription status, processed by our payment provider.
- **Usage:** device, language, pages visited inside the app.

## 2. How we use it

- To show you your cards, installments, and payment projections.
- To send due-date reminders, if you enabled them.
- To process your subscription and keep your plan up to date.
- To improve the app based on aggregated usage.

## 3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with providers needed to operate the service (payment processor, hosting, email delivery), under confidentiality agreements.

## 4. Security

Your password is stored encrypted. We never store your full credit card number or security code — that stays with the payment processor.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and also to export the information you entered. Write to us and we will handle it within a reasonable time.

## 6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, and optional analytics cookies to understand how the app is used. You can turn the latter off in your browser settings.

## 7. Contact

For any privacy question, write to us at privacidad@cuotas.app.
